Your passwords are never sent anywhere, because VaultWiz has nothing to send them to. They live in one encrypted file on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, and nowhere else.

A wheel of cards

Turn your logins like a wheel.

Every login is a colourful card. Drag the wheel, tap a card or pick a letter. The side wheel is free, and VaultWiz Plus adds five more styles. On iPad and Mac you can switch to a plain list.

See what needs care

Find the weak spots, fix them one by one.

VaultWiz finds short passwords and passwords you use on more than one login, then walks you through making new ones. It keeps a draft until you save, so nothing is lost halfway.

One-time codes

Your two-step codes, on the same card.

Paste a key, scan a QR code or pick a picture. The code sits with its login, counting down, ready to copy. Adding codes is part of VaultWiz Plus; codes you saved keep working whatever happens.

Generator

A strong new password in one tap.

Make passwords, passphrases that are easy to type, or usernames, then start a new login with it in one tap.

AutoFill

Fills your logins in Safari and other apps on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Part of VaultWiz Plus.

Face ID and Touch ID

Unlock with a glance or a touch, with auto-lock when you step away.

Encrypted backups

A clipboard that forgets

Made for each device

Your keys, your file

Locked with your password, and only yours.

The whole vault is one encrypted file. Without your master password it is just noise, on your device, in a backup or anywhere else.

AES-256-GCMEncrypts everything in the vault, and notices if the file was changed.
scryptTurns your master password into the key, and makes every guess slow and costly.
On deviceNo account and no copy on a server. Breach checks, sharing and sync are left out on purpose, because they need a network.
No trackingNo analytics, no ads, no third-party code. The App Store privacy label reads “Data Not Collected”.

On the Mac too

The same cards, at home on your desk.

A native Mac app with the wheel, or a plain list if you prefer, and AutoFill for your logins. Each device keeps its own vault; move it with an encrypted backup when you want to.

Good to know

The honest small print.

No password reset

Nobody can recover a forgotten master password, not even the developer. Keep an encrypted backup and remember the password.

Device backups

iCloud Backup or Time Machine may include the vault file. It stays encrypted there.

Not audited yet

VaultWiz has not been independently audited yet. Questions are welcome on the support page.
