VaultWiz privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

VaultWiz collects nothing. It has no account, server, analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party code. The developer cannot see your vault, or know that you use the app beyond what Apple reports in general App Store statistics.

What stays on your device

Your logins, notes and one-time-code keys are kept in one encrypted file on your device. VaultWiz has no network code of its own, so it cannot send them anywhere.

Your device's own backups, such as iCloud Backup or Time Machine, may include that file. It stays encrypted there, and you control those backups in your system settings. A backup you export yourself goes wherever you choose to save it, and is encrypted too.

VaultWiz Plus

The subscription is sold and handled by Apple. VaultWiz receives no payment details and no personal details. To know whether Plus is active, the app reads the signed purchase record Apple keeps on your device. Nothing about it goes into your vault, and nothing from your vault goes to Apple through the purchase.

Permissions

  • Face ID or Touch ID: only to unlock your vault. VaultWiz never sees your face or fingerprint data.
  • Camera: only to read a QR code when you add a one-time code. Nothing is recorded or sent.

AutoFill

To suggest logins, VaultWiz gives the system's AutoFill the website and user name of each login. Passwords are never included. This stays on your device, and you can turn it off in VaultWiz's Settings.

Children

VaultWiz collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.
