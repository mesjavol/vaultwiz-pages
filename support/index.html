I forgot my master password

It can't be recovered. VaultWiz has no account or server, and nobody else holds a copy. If you remember the password of an older backup, you can restore that backup from the lock screen.

How do I back up my vault?

Choose Export backup… (under Folders on iPhone, in the sidebar on iPad and Mac) and save the file somewhere safe. A backup keeps the master password it was made with, so make a new one after changing your password.

How do I move to a new device?

Save a backup on the old device, move the file yourself, then restore it from the lock screen on the new device. VaultWiz doesn't sync.

What does VaultWiz Plus add?

Unlimited items (the free plan holds 25), AutoFill, adding one-time codes and all six wheel styles. It costs $19.99 a year or $3.99 a month, with a 7-day free trial. Prices may vary by region.

How do I cancel?

In VaultWiz, go to Settings > Manage subscription. You can also cancel in your Apple account's subscription settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period to avoid the next charge. Apple handles refunds, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens when a subscription ends?

You can still open, view, edit and export everything, including items above 25, and saved one-time codes keep working. Adding new items above 25, adding new codes, AutoFill and the extra wheel styles stop. Subscribe again and they come back as they were.

I subscribed but it shows the free plan

Go to Settings > Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple account.

How do I turn on AutoFill?

AutoFill is part of VaultWiz Plus. On iPhone and iPad, turn on Face ID or Touch ID in VaultWiz's Settings, then go to Settings > General > AutoFill & Passwords and turn on VaultWiz. On Mac, turn it on in System Settings > General > AutoFill & Passwords.

Why can't AutoFill on my iPhone take my master password?

Checking a master password needs more memory than iOS allows AutoFill to use, so on iPhone and iPad it unlocks only with Face ID or Touch ID.

Why is a login not offered on a website?

VaultWiz offers a login only when the site matches the one you saved exactly, or is a subdomain of it. That stops look-alike sites from getting your password.
